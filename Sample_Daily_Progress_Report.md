### 4. Resource Monitoring

The daily report may also capture:

- Labour deployment by trade.
- Material consumption during the reporting period.
- Equipment availability and utilisation.
- Material receipt and site inventory updates.

### 5. Site Observations

- Record progress against planned construction activities.
- Identify activities experiencing delays or resource constraints.
- Document material availability and coordination requirements.
- Highlight issues requiring attention from the project team.

### 6. Purpose of DPR

A Daily Progress Report supports construction planning and execution by providing a structured record of daily activities, resource deployment and progress against planned targets.
