# PHOTOGPRAHS – DLF Ltd. Planning Internship

## Overview

This folder contains a curated collection of photographs documenting my on-site exposure during my internship with the Planning Department at **DLF Ltd.**

The photographs provide a visual record of selected site visits and construction activities observed during the internship.

## Project Exposure

My site exposure included the following developments:

- **Downtown Towers 5, 6, 7 & 8**
- **Mall of India Phase 2, Gurugram**

## Photograph Categories

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Site Visits | Photographs documenting my presence at construction sites |
| Construction Activities | Selected site activities observed during the internship |
| Project Exposure | Visual documentation of construction environments and execution stages |

## Key Learning Areas

The site visits provided practical exposure to:

- Construction execution and site operations.
- Progress monitoring and coordination.
- Construction activity sequencing.
- Material utilisation and site management.
- The relationship between project planning and on-site execution.

## Purpose

These photographs complement the internship documentation by providing visual context to my practical exposure to large-scale real estate construction projects.
